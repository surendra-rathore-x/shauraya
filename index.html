ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Learning today. Leading tomorrow.

The Royal Rajput International School is a modern co-educational institution committed to providing quality education in a safe, inspiring and student-friendly environment.

We focus on academic excellence along with sports, creativity, technology, communication skills, leadership and character development.

Our Vision

“To create confident, knowledgeable and responsible individuals who are prepared to make a positive difference in the world.”

PRINCIPAL'S MESSAGE

Dr. Kritika Shekhawat

“Welcome to The Royal Rajput International School.”

Our aim is to create an environment where every student feels valued, encouraged and inspired to learn. We believe education is not limited to textbooks and examinations; it is about developing curiosity, confidence, creativity, discipline and strong values.

Together with our dedicated teachers and supportive parents, we strive to prepare our students to become responsible and successful citizens of tomorrow.

Dr. Kritika Shekhawat Principal
